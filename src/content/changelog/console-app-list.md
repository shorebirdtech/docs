---
title: Find apps faster in the Console
date: 2026-09-23
area: Console
type: New
---

The sidebar now lists the active organization's apps, and the organization's app
list can now be filtered, sorted, and paged.

- The sidebar shows up to 10 apps with their icons, with the open app and the
  ones you visited recently first.
- On the app list, filter by platform and sort by last updated, created date, or
  name. Long lists are split into pages.
