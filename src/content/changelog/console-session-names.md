---
title: Sessions are named after the app that holds them
date: 2026-10-12
area: Console
type: Changed
---

The Sessions list on your Account page now names each session after the app that
signed in, such as Shorebird CLI or Claude Code, instead of an internal ID like
`shorebird-cli (api)`.

- Sessions from CLIs older than 1.6.125 keep the browser name they had.
- Renamed apps rename their existing sessions too.
