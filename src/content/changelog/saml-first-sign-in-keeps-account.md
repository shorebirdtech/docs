title: First SSO sign-in keeps your existing account
date: 2026-10-17
area: Console

docLink:
  label: How sign-in works
  href: /enterprise/saml/#how-sign-in-works
---

Signing in with SAML SSO for the first time with an email at the connection's
domain moves your existing account onto SSO instead of refusing it. Your
organizations, app access, and billing carry over.

- The account's Google or Microsoft sign-in is replaced, and its existing
  sessions are signed out.
