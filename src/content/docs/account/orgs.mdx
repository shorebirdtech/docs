Organizations are a way to share groups of apps with people. This is useful for
any team collaborating across a company to have access as needed to releases,
patches, and more.

## Account requirements

Shorebird's paid plans unlock features for collaborating with your peers.
Shorebird's free plan users can upgrade to a paid plan to access these features.
See [Plans & Upgrading](/account/plans/) for how.

If you do not see the plan you're expecting, please reach out to
contact@shorebird.dev for help.

## Creating an organization

To create an organization, open the Shorebird console and click the
organization's name at the top of the sidebar. In the switcher that opens, click
"New organization", enter a name, and click "Create organization". You can
rename it later in the organization's settings.

Creating an organization needs a paid plan of your own. On the Free plan, the
switcher shows "Upgrade to create an organization" instead.

## Managing apps

Organizations can have multiple apps associated with them. You can either
transfer existing apps to an organization or create new apps within the
organization directly.

### Create a new app

To create a new app within an organization, select the organization when running
`shorebird init`:

<Image
  src={initWithOrg}
  alt="Screenshot of doing `shorebird init` command and selecting an organization"
/>

### Transfer an app

To transfer an existing app to an organization, visit the app's settings and
click the "Transfer" button beside the "Transfer Ownership" section. You can
then select the organization you want to transfer the app to from the dropdown.
You must be an admin or owner of both the app's current organization and the one
you transfer it to. Organizations where you aren't are shown in the dropdown but
can't be selected, and if you aren't an admin or owner of the app's current
organization, the "Transfer" button is disabled.

The app's releases, patches and collaborators move with it, and collaborators
keep their access. Members of the old organization lose access to the app unless
they are also in the new one or are collaborators on the app. Their API keys
stop working for the app too, so update any CI that uses one.

Usage follows the app's current organization. All of the app's installs in the
new organization owner's current billing period, including installs from before
the transfer, count toward that owner's usage and plan limits. Check that the
new owner's plan has room before transferring a high-traffic app.

<Image
  src={transferAppToDifferentOrg}
  alt="Screenshot of dialog for transferring app to a different org in the Shorebird Console"
/>

You can also transfer an app from the command line with
[`shorebird apps transfer`](/account/cli#transfer-an-app).

### Remove a member

To remove a member, open the organization's settings and go to the "Members"
section. Click the ⋮ menu next to the member, choose "Remove", and confirm.

### Accept an invitation

When someone invites you to an organization, Shorebird emails you a link to
accept it. You can also see and answer your pending invitations from the command
line:

```sh
shorebird invitations list
```

Each line shows the invitation's id, the organization or app name, its kind, the
role you'd get, who sent it, and when it was sent and expires. Accept or decline
one by its id:

```sh
shorebird invitations accept <id>
shorebird invitations decline <id>
```

Sign in as the invited email address first. If you're signed in as a different
account, the CLI tells you to run `shorebird account whoami` and
`shorebird login`. Service account keys and release and patch only API keys
can't answer invitations.

`shorebird invitations` needs Shorebird 1.6.130 or newer.

## Member roles

Organization roles apply to all apps within the organization and can be managed
via the organization settings page in the Shorebird console. The roles you can
give members depend on the organization's plan, which is its owner's plan.

|             | Free | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
| ----------- | ---- | --- | -------- | ---------- |
| Viewer      |      | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| Developer   | ✅   | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| App Manager |      |     |          | ✅         |
| Admin       |      | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| Owner       | ✅   | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |

[Service accounts](/account/service-accounts/#choose-a-role) hold roles too,
with their own choices: Viewer, Developer, App Manager, or Auditor, on every
plan.

### Viewer

Read-only access for stakeholders who need visibility into apps, releases, and
patches without the ability to make changes. Ideal for product managers, QA,
support, or leadership teams.

### Developer

Hands-on role for engineers actively building and shipping apps. Can create
releases and patches, promote patches, and manage day-to-day development
workflows without administrative access.

### App manager

Responsible for managing application lifecycle and release strategy. Has all
Developer capabilities plus elevated control over app-level configuration,
environments, and release coordination.

### Admin

Administrative role for managing team access and organization-level settings.
Can configure apps, manage users and roles, and handle most operational tasks,
excluding billing and plan changes.

### Owner

Full control over the organization. Can manage billing and plans, ownership
settings, and all platform capabilities. Intended for primary account holders.

## Billing & ownership

Each organization has a single owner. An organization's plan is determined by
its [owner's](#owner) subscription. Their Shorebird subscription is what sets
the plan and available features for the entire organization.

To manage billing, the organization owner can visit the
[billing settings](https://console.shorebird.dev/account) in the Shorebird
console. See [Billing](/account/billing) for more details.

## Per-app collaborators

Shorebird no longer adds new per-app collaborators. To give someone access to an
app, [add them as a member](#add-a-member) of the organization that owns it. A
member's role applies to every app the organization owns.

- If the app is in your personal account, which can't have members,
  [create an organization](#creating-an-organization),
  [transfer](#transfer-an-app) the app into it, and invite people there.
- To share some apps but not others, create a separate organization for them,
  transfer those apps into it, and invite people there.

Existing collaborators keep their access for now. From the app's "Collaborators"
tab you can still change a collaborator's role, remove them from the ⋮ menu next
to their email address, and revoke invitations that haven't been accepted. If
you were invited to an app before this change, you can still
[accept the invitation](#accept-an-invitation).
